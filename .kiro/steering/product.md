# Product steering — Kiroween 2026 Frankenstein entry

## What this is
A read-only web app that triages free NFT mint contracts on BNB Chain. A user pastes a
contract address; the app checks it three ways and shows a plain risk verdict with the
raw evidence. It is a triage tool, not a safety guarantee and not investment advice.

## Who it is for
People who hunt free NFT mints (like the builder) and want a fast first-pass check
before they look deeper — not a substitute for reading the contract or the project.

## Non-goals (hard boundaries)
- No wallet connection. No signing. No transactions. No spending. Ever.
- No promises: the app must never say a contract is "safe". The best verdict is
  "clean" meaning "no flags found by these three checks".
- No price predictions, no returns, no "buy/mint now" language.
- No referral links, no tracking, no accounts.

## Tone
Plain, honest, short. Numbers come from live reads and are labelled with the block
height / time they were read at. Unknowns are stated as unknowns.
